## Questions and ideas: Discussions

For "how do I…?", "does it work with my machine?" or "what if…?", use
[Discussions](https://github.com/jahziel1/tlaku/discussions). Please search first: your question may
already have an answer.

## Read the manual first

- [User manual (English)](docs/manual/en/00-indice.md): first steps, connecting a machine, sending
  safely, materials, [troubleshooting](docs/manual/en/15-solucion-de-problemas.md).
- [User manual (Español)](docs/manual/00-indice.md).
- The manual is also inside the app: Help > User Manual (with search).

## Reports

Use the right form at [Issues](https://github.com/jahziel1/tlaku/issues/new/choose):

| You want to… | Form |
|---|---|
| Report something that does not work | [Bug report](https://github.com/jahziel1/tlaku/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) |
| Tell us how Tlaku worked on your real machine | [Real machine test report](https://github.com/jahziel1/tlaku/issues/new?template=machine_report.yml) |
| Share material settings that worked for you | [Share material settings](https://github.com/jahziel1/tlaku/issues/new?template=material_settings.yml) |
| Ask for a new controller | [New controller support](https://github.com/jahziel1/tlaku/issues/new?template=new_controller.yml) |
| Propose a feature | [Feature request](https://github.com/jahziel1/tlaku/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) |

The Help menu of the app can open these forms already filled in with your version, system and
machine. Nothing is sent without your review.

## Security problems

**Do not use public issues or discussions** for vulnerabilities or anything that could fire the laser
where it should not, fail to stop it, or move the machine unexpectedly. Report it privately: see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## About this project

Tlaku is made by volunteers and has no commercial support. Tlaku has only been tested with emulators:
treat every job as a test and keep the emergency stop within reach. The project follows its
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
